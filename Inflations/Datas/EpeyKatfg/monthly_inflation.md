## Method

- Monthly price per product = **mean of its daily lowest prices** in the month (products observed on ≥ 5 days).
- Consecutive months are matched on stable product ID (`UrunID`, same TUIK group).
- `avg_inflation` = Carli index: arithmetic mean of per-product monthly relatives.
- `basic_index` = Dutot index: (Σp_m / Σp_{m−1} − 1) × 100.
- `tuik_weighted` = two-stage Carli: per-group mean relative, weighted by TÜİK 2026 weights **normalised to 100% over the covered groups** (we do not cover the full TÜİK basket).
- Weighted groups: 03, 05, 06, 08, 09, 13 (covered basket weight = 30.551%). TUIK 07 (tyres) is excluded from weighting (< 20 matched products; `--min-group-n 20`).
- Products with |monthly relative| > 80% are dropped as scraping artefacts.
